Suggested order

  • First: option 2 polish and option 5 refund-to-source. Both sit on the bank feed you already have (record_bank_transaction, refund_claims) and remove the two places people type or upload things by hand.
  • Second: option 1 with option 3. Private events with pay at the door are the lowest-risk way for a new host to start, and they are what the Partiful-style link is for.
  • Third: option 4 for hosts who get no-shows, then the rest of option 6.
  • One rule stays in every option: money goes guest to host directly and banbe never holds it. Holding funds would make banbe a payment intermediary, which needs a State Bank licence. A lawyer should confirm before anything that looks like escrow.

What the research said

A transfer screenshot proves nothingApps now produce a fake "transfer successful" bill for any bank in one tap, and banks cannot help afterwards because nothing in the picture is real. So no option here accepts a screenshot. Only the bank feed or the host's own bank app counts. VnExpress
Banks now warn before a transferThe State Bank's SIMO system shares about 592,000 suspected accounts, and bank apps show a warning when the receiver is on the list. One bank reported 95,500 customers cancelling after a warning. A host account that triggers this should be paused on banbe too. Tuổi Trẻ · VnEconomy
Face scan from 10 million ₫Transfers of 10 million ₫ or more, or over 20 million ₫ a day, need biometric confirmation in the bank app. Normal tickets stay far below, large group orders may not, so the pay screen should say so. Việt Nam News
One QR sold to several peopleThe usual ticket scam is a resale in a Facebook group where the same QR goes to many buyers, or a fake "organizer" email. A QR that changes every 30 seconds and an in-app hand-over to a named friend close this. VietNamNet · VnExpress
Partiful: private by default"Who can find this event?" starts as Private and public is a choice. Its older "Chip In" links out to payment apps and cannot verify anyone paid, which is why it added real ticketing in June 2026 with payout after the event. Partiful help · Chip In vs tickets
Door sales in the same listCheck-in guides agree on two things: walk-up payments must land in the same live count as online tickets, and every code must reject a second scan. Option 3 does both. TickPick · Eventbrite
Refund back to the paying accountVietQR's own refund service works on the original transaction: check it, then refund it. The same idea is used here without banbe moving money: the host pays a pre-filled code addressed to the account the money came from. VietQR refund API
Fake QR codes over ZaloBanks warn about QR codes and links sent through Zalo, Facebook and Viber. So the invite link must be visibly banbe.app, and banbe should warn when a chat message asks to pay somewhere else. Việt Nam News
