Decisions made

  • Decided, 16 Nov: the pay screen opens the bank app first. A guest's first payment gets the guided three steps. The QR is a sheet behind "Show QR instead" on both, for paying from another phone.
  • Decided, 16 Nov: photo proof is removed completely, for payments and for refunds. An optional bank reference number takes its place. Photos already uploaded stay readable until their booking or dispute is closed.
  • Decided, 16 Nov: one refund rule for every event, set by banbe and written in the Terms. Hosts do not choose. The steps: 100% back more than 48 hours before, 50% from 48 to 24 hours, nothing under 24 hours (the guest can give the ticket to a friend), and 100% whenever the host cancels. A lawyer should check these against consumer-protection rules before they go into the Terms.
  • Decided, 16 Nov: keep the payer's bank, name and account number from the bank feed, and offer it first for refunds. Shown masked only, to that guest and to the host for that one refund. Needs a line in the Terms (section B2). Saved refund accounts stay as the fallback when the bank does not share it.
  • Decided, 16 Nov: a host with an overdue refund cannot publish a new event until it is sent. Their existing events and tickets are not touched. The guest can also ask banbe to step in.

Not verified yet

  • Whether Casso and PayOS send the payer's account for every bank. I believe both have fields for it and that some banks leave them empty, which is why the prototype has a "bank did not share it" path. This needs a look at real webhook data.
  • Whether your feed provider reports outgoing transfers for every host bank. Casso reports them as negative amounts (the webhook code says so). PayOS payment links may not.
  • Bank app links differ per bank and per phone. The QR and copy fields stay as the fallback in every layout.
